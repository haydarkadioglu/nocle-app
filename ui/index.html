How to use Nocle

Nocle uses a deep learning model to remove background noise from audio recordings. Follow the steps below to clean up your files.

Steps

  1. Browse — click the Browse button and select a .wav, .mp3, .flac, .ogg, .aac or .m4a file.
  2. Configure filters — optionally enable one or more filters in the sidebar.
  3. Process — click Process and wait. Progress is shown in real time.
  4. Compare — play back both the original and processed versions using the player cards.
  5. Save — when satisfied, click Save to export the cleaned audio as a .wav file.

Filters

Spectral Gate
Suppresses static background noise (fans, air conditioning) by attenuating frequencies below a dynamic threshold.
Wiener Filter
A mathematical filter that estimates and reduces random noise across the frequency spectrum. Increase Wiener Size for stronger reduction (may cause muddiness).
Gaussian Blur
Smooths sharp transients and click-like artefacts. Gaussian Sigma controls the smoothing radius.
Normalize Output
Scales the processed audio so its peak level is −3 dBFS, preventing the result from being too quiet or too loud.

Tips

  • The AI model alone is often sufficient — try it without any filters first.
  • Enable Show Spectrograms to see a frequency-domain view of the before and after.
  • Your filter settings are saved automatically and restored on next launch.
